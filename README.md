Grama-Sethu 🌉

Rural Bridge Monitoring and Flood Alert System

Grama-Sethu is a real-time Android application developed to improve rural travel safety during monsoon seasons. The application helps users monitor bridge conditions, receive flood and weather alerts, report damaged or submerged bridges, and access emergency support.

The project focuses on helping rural communities, travelers, students, farmers, and emergency services avoid dangerous bridge crossings during heavy rainfall.

🎯 Problem Statement

Small bridges and culverts in rural areas often become submerged or damaged during monsoon seasons. Travelers usually do not have access to real-time bridge condition updates before reaching the location. This may lead to dangerous travel situations, delays, accidents, and isolation of villages.

Grama-Sethu solves this problem by providing live bridge monitoring, weather-based travel warnings, community reporting, and emergency alerts through a mobile application.
